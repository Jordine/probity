# Interpreting the completed display screen

All 24 families contribute seven complete branches with an identical original first round and private plans. Every policy then receives four freshly generated suffix arguments with contextual probe scoring between actions. Results therefore compare these continuations conditional on that prefix; they do not estimate effects of feedback from the beginning of a wholly new debate.

The full bundle reduces exact self-repetition by 12.5 percentage points against no feedback (95% family-bootstrap interval −18.75 to −6.25), but only 4.17 points against the neutral packet (−11.46 to +3.13). Its mean response-length change is −8.82 words against none (−20.95 to +2.33) and +3.52 against neutral (−5.87 to +13.28). Receiving an additional feedback-shaped context can explain part of the observed behavior change; a difference from no feedback alone does not isolate useful numeric information.

The incorrect-assigned-role probe percentile rises, rather than falls, under the bundle: +3.13 points against none (+1.22 to +5.25) and +2.95 against neutral (+1.00 to +4.86). These are contextual response-level role-proxy signals, not local lie probabilities. We cannot infer truthfulness from their direction. The separate source-based sentence labels address factuality.

Haiku judged 21/24 bundle branches correctly versus 19/24 none and 19/24 neutral. Native Qwen3.5-4B judged 18/24 bundle and none branches correctly versus 17/24 neutral. These small descriptive counts do not establish that one display is best. The completed paired two-judge analysis is in `../live_judges_v1/report.md`.

The shuffled packet is a weak control: 23/96 numeric packets are unchanged by its permutation, and 47/96 detector-only recommendations match the unshuffled recommendation for that same prior history. It must not be described as guaranteed random misinformation.

The two hash-selected qualitative readings show persistence of the false assigned account. In case 1, bundle Red drops one invented quotation but retains another, and neutral produces a similar change. In case 2, bundle Red reinterprets an authentic anniversary clue rather than conceding. Neither case establishes a general causal mechanism. Both complete readings and all seven-policy raw request packets are available alongside this file.

Twelve freshly generated arguments in case 2 are demonstrably incomplete in the raw provider output despite normal stop and ample token allowance. The primary analysis retains all branches. A separate post-hoc sensitivity removes the entire affected family across all seven conditions, retaining 23 families. A four-call diagnostic supports an output-format association for two selected contexts but does not identify provider internals or replace production transcripts.

The frozen historical B feedback remains untouched. Separate runtime auditing found that some replayed historical displayed percentiles differ slightly; final counts belong to the full GPU audit. Exact same-process original/shared scoring equivalence was separately verified before generation resumed.
